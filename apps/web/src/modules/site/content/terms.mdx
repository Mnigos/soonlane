Pro costs $5 a month or $48 a year, plus tax. Tax is calculated and added at checkout, and checkout shows the final amount before you pay. Under the [Sold Through Link Terms](https://link.com/terms), checkout may show the price in your local currency, which Link converts at an exchange rate that includes a markup. Your subscription renews automatically at the end of each month or year until it is cancelled, and each renewal is charged to the same payment method. Each renewal is the same $5 or $48 plus tax, though when you pay in your local currency the amount of each renewal in that currency can differ.

You can cancel at any time with "Manage subscription" in the account menu, or in your Link account at [link.com](https://link.com), where you can also see orders, update your subscription and change your payment method (if you checked out as a guest, Link asks you to create a Link account for this). After cancelling, Pro stays active until the end of the period already paid for, then ends, and there are no more charges.
